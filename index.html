How these charts are built
  • Chip-EV equilibrium for a table where everyone has your stack. Blinds are 0.5 and 1, with no antes.
  • Use the smaller of your stack and the stack of whoever can call you. If a short stack is behind you, look up their stack instead.
  • Only the first caller is modelled. Multiway all-ins are ignored, so late-position ranges run a touch wide.
  • No payout structure. Near the money, tighten up a little.
  • Block times come from the app's blind list and can drift against the room. Check the real clock if a level feels early or late.
  • Heads-up checks: at 10bb the small blind shoves 58% and the big blind calls 38%. At 5bb it is 72% and 62%.
