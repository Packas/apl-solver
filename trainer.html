How the answers are decided
  • The charts come from a push/fold solver for a table where everyone has the same stack. Blinds are 0.5 and 1 with no antes.
  • The answer uses the solved stack closest to yours (1.5 to 20 big blinds). Past 20, shoving is not the play.
  • A hand the chart plays between about 25% and 75% of the time counts as a close call. Either answer is marked correct.
  • Hands near the edge of a range come up more often, since those are the ones worth learning.
  • Only the first caller is modelled and payouts are ignored. Real opponents are looser or tighter than a chart.
